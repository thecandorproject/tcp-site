How to check a version yourself

Once you've downloaded the file, click "Checksum" next to the entry to copy its hash, then run this command in a terminal (Mac/Linux: shasum -a 256 filename.pdf, Windows: certutil -hashfile filename.pdf SHA256) and compare the result to it. If they match exactly, your copy is identical to the published file.
