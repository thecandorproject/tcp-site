Change Log

A record of changes to The Candor Project's published documents and framework. Additions expand the framework; revisions modify existing content. The rationale for each substantive change is noted.

June 2026
  • Addition Published Methodology and Evidentiary Standards, the operational companion to the white paper. The methodology document defines the evidentiary standards, exclusion categories, knowledge requirements, and appeals process that govern every finding the oversight body produces. Its publication completes the Phase 1 document set.
  • Revision White paper, Oversight Body Architecture section: replaced "legally admissible public record" with "formally documented and publicly verifiable record." The original phrase implied a specific procedural context the framework does not yet occupy. The revised language accurately describes the oversight body's output and is consistent with the terminology used throughout the methodology document.
April 2026
  • Addition Initial publication of the framework. Published documents: Epistemic Accountability and the Constitution: A Framework for Professional Truth Standards in Public Life (white paper); The Missing Standard: A Professional Accountability Framework for Politicians and the Press (public explainer); and the one-pager.
